About

I'm Dio — an illustrator & graphic designer bridging visual art with technology.

For over five years I've been exploring the intersection of Art × Tech, weaving surrealist aesthetics and cosmic landscapes into character-driven digital artworks.

Every piece starts as a fragment — a face, a planet, a doorway — and grows into a small mythology of its own. I make work for collectors and art enthusiasts who want to feel like they've stepped into another world.

  • Surrealism Dreamlike figures & symbolism
  • Cosmic Worlds Planets, portals & landscapes
  • Character Design Authentic, story-driven figures
  • Digital Craft Illustration built with intent
